Tennis stocking fillers

The best tennis stocking fillers are small, useful and a little bit fun: a dampener pair (£7.99), a pack of overgrips (£11.99), a tube of balls or a wristband. Our dampeners are 3g of silicone in a holographic pouch, so they slip into a stocking and look the part when they come out.

A dampener sits between the strings to soften vibration and the ping off the racket. Ours come two to a pack, so there's one for them and one for their doubles partner.
